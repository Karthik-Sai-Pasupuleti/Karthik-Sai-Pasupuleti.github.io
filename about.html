About

I'm Karthik Sai Pasupuleti — a machine-learning engineer and PhD researcher using AI to make roads safer, now building vision-language-action models with spiking neural networks.

Bio

Making Roads Safer
with Intelligent Systems

My goal is simple: use intelligent systems to save lives on the road. From testing L4 autonomous vehicles at Momenta, to building collision-avoidance and trajectory prediction at SANEON, to driver-safety and crash-prediction deep learning at VAIVA — every project has been driven by that mission.

Now, as a PhD researcher at AImotion Bavaria (THI Ingolstadt), I work on a new frontier: vision-language-action (VLA) models built with spiking neural networks — brain-inspired systems that perceive, reason about, and act in complex traffic scenes, designed to run efficiently on neuromorphic hardware like Intel Loihi.
